How to use

  • RegExp Pattern: A regular expression to match the filename or URL. (e.g. \.pdf$ or unsplash.com)
  • Folder: The folder inside your Downloads directory.
  • Variables: You can use {year}, {month}, {monthName}, {day} in folder names.
    Example: {year}/{month}-{monthName} → 2024/12-Dec
